Frequently Asked Questions

Getting covered with Pinnacle Shield is straightforward: start by requesting a quote online, review the plan options tailored to your needs, select your coverage, and submit your application — our team will confirm your policy within one business day. The answers below address the most common questions along the way.

Pinnacle Shield offers auto, home, and life insurance. Each product includes three coverage tiers — Basic, Standard, and Premium — so you can match your level of protection to your budget and needs.

Your premium is based on a base rate for your insurance type, adjusted by factors specific to your situation. For auto insurance those factors include your age, vehicle, mileage, and driving record. Home insurance considers your home's value, age, construction, and safety features. Life insurance accounts for your age, health, and coverage amount.

Your auto rate is influenced by your age, the year and make of your vehicle, your annual mileage, and your driving record. Younger drivers and those with recent tickets or accidents typically pay higher rates, while low-mileage drivers with clean records qualify for the best pricing.

Standard coverage is our most popular tier and includes a well-rounded set of protections at the base rate. Premium coverage offers the broadest protection and highest benefit limits at ×1.4 of the standard rate.
