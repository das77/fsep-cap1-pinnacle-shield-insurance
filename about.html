About Pinnacle Shield Insurance

Founded in 1985 and headquartered in Hartford, CT, Pinnacle Shield Insurance has spent four decades building a reputation for reliability, transparency, and customer-first service. What began as a small regional provider has grown into a nationally recognized insurance company trusted by over two million policyholders.

Today, Pinnacle Shield serves customers in all 50 states, offering a full suite of coverage options including auto, home, and life insurance. Our network of licensed agents and 24/7 support team ensures that help is always available — whether you're filing a claim at midnight or shopping for a new policy on a Sunday afternoon.

We believe that protecting what matters most shouldn't be complicated or costly. That's why we combine competitive rates, flexible plan options, and fast claims processing to deliver peace of mind to individuals and families across the country. At Pinnacle Shield, you're not just a policy number — you're a neighbor we're proud to look out for.
